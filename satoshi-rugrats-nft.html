🍼 About the Collection

Satoshi Rugrats is an NFT collection born from the intersection of crypto culture and 90s nostalgia. Each of the 6 unique characters embodies a distinct archetype from the blockchain world — from the visionary founder to the diamond-hand HODLer, the proof-of-work miner, the proof-of-stake validator, the market whale, and the DeFi developer.

Built on the ERC-721 standard, every Satoshi Rugrat token carries rich on-chain metadata including character traits, crypto roles, accessories, and rarity tiers. Own a piece of the decentralized playground.
